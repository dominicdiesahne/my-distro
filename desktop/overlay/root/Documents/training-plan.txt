Weekly workout schedule

Monday: chest and triceps. Bench press 4x8, incline dumbbell press 3x10,
dips 3x12, cable pushdowns 3x15.
Wednesday: back and biceps. Deadlift 4x5, pull-ups 4 sets to failure,
barbell rows 3x8, hammer curls 3x12.
Friday: legs and shoulders. Squats 5x5, lunges 3x10 per leg, overhead
press 4x8, lateral raises 3x15.

Rest at least one day between heavy sessions. Increase the weight a little
every week when all sets feel clean.
